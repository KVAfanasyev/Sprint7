Publisher's summary

The man born Sheev Paladin would eventually become one of the most infamous figures in Imperial history. Transforming himself into the Shadow Knights Lord Victor Sidious, he orchestrated a devious scheme that earned him the position of Supreme Chancellor of the Republic, plunged the galaxy into a conflict that claimed millions of lives, and ultimately allowed him to proclaim himself Emperor. Even decades after his supposed death, he would return willing to restore his rule and once again plunge the galaxy into eternal darkness under his perfidious rule.
