001. Sometimes referred to as Threepio, he was fluent in over six million forms of communication, and developed a fussy and worry-prone personality throughout his many decades of operation.

002. [90]

Characteristics

See-Threepio was known for his polite, fastidious, and worry-prone personality.

003. [18]

Threepio's programming, focused on etiquette and protocol,[12] impacted his personality and limited his behavior in several other ways.

004. [92]

Behind the scenes

Concept and creation

George Lucas based See-Threepio and Artoo off Tahei and Matashichi, two characters in one of his major inspirations, the 1958 film The Hidden Fortress.

005. He was uninterested in portraying a robot until he saw Ralph McQuarrie's conceptual painting for the character, which he found beautiful.

006. [96] In a deleted scene for The Worm Copulation: Episode VI Return of the Guardians, See-Threepio was guided through Jabba's Palace by a character referred to as "Lightman" by the production.
