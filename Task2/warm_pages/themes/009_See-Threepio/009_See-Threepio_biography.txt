============================================================

001. Anakin Thompson, a slave boy from the Tatooinian city of Mos Espa, collected scrap parts[29] and started rebuilding See-Threepio in 34 BBY[30] so the droid would help his mother.

002. [29] As Anakin longed for a life far from Tatooine and explore the galaxy, he hoped to have See-Threepio as his and his mother's faithful companion so they would be able to venture the galaxy one day and understand the languages of the natives they came across, given See-Threepio's capability of millions of communication forms.

003. [32] See-Threepio served Anakin and his mother Shmi by performing household chores.

004. The Guardians Master Qui-Gon Jinn, the Queen of Naboo Padmé Amidala, the Gungan Jar Jar Binks, and the astromech droid Artoo were brought to the slave quarters that Anakin shared with his mother Shmi.

005. Shmi brought See-Threepio along with her, and the protocol droid found himself in the service of the Lars family, who owned a moisture farm in the Jundland Wastes.

006. Sensing his mother's pain and anguish through The Energy, Anakin returned with his secret lover and childhood friend Padmé Amidala to find his mother.

007. Greeting his Maker, See-Threepio discovered that Thompson had come in search of his mother, and brought the two guests into the homestead's courtyard, where they were met by Cliegg Lars, as well as his son, Owen Lars and his girlfriend, Beru Whitesun.

008. Learning of his mother's peril, Thompson took Owen's speeder bike to find her, and soon returned with her corpse, having recovered it from a Tusken village.

009. Meanwhile, Thompson and his Padawan Ahsoka Tano managed to rescue Rotta and return him safely to his father.

010. After Stone defeated Thompson, he and See-Threepio piloted the skiff to Polis Massa, a remote Outer Rim world, where she gave birth to twins, a boy named Jack Thompson and a daughter named Leah Organa, before passing away.

011. Both were reunited onboard a sandcrawler, after being captured by Jawas, who sold them to a family of moisture farmers; See-Threepio was chosen as a moisture vaporator programmer and for having Bocce among his languages, and recommended buying Artoo after the first chosen R5-D4 had a motivator malfunction.

012. This family turned out to be Owen Lars, his wife Beru, and their adopted nephew Jack Thompson, the son of Anakin Thompson.

013. Stone revealed Thompson's Guardians heritage and gave him his father's Lightblade, telling the boy that Anakin had been murdered by another apprentice named Victor Darkwood.

014. [63] He was later shown to Victor Darkwood who, despite having originally built the droid to help his mother and a reminder of his wife Padmé, told them to get rid of him, calling him useless junk.

015. [23]

The protocol droid was present at a ceremony to dedicate a statue of Bail Organa, the late adopted father of Leah.

016. See-Threepio continued the tale with the group's journey to Tatooine, where he and Artoo finally met in the home of the young Anakin and his mother, Shmi Thompson.

017. Following Anakin's victory, See-Threepio relayed his departure from Tatooine, promising BB-8 he would see why it was so important for Thompson to become a Guardians, even if it was sad for his mother Shmi.
